# VibeSync Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 2026

---

## Introduction

**VibeSync** values user privacy and is committed to safeguarding personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines the information we collect, how it is used to deliver synchronized watch and listen experiences, and how we protect your privacy.

*Tagline:* **"Feel the Beat, Together. Watch YouTube videos in sync with friends."**

---

## Information Collected

To provide real-time room synchronization, custom user profiles, and personalized favorites, VibeSync collects and processes:

- **Account Information:** Email address, display name, unique handle (`@username`), and selected predefined avatar.
- **Room & Synchronization Data:** Room ID (`VIBE-XXXX`), room title, currently playing media details, and real-time playback control events (play, pause, seek, media changes).
- **User Preferences & History:** Saved favorite media items, custom watch history, and user application settings (e.g., Data Saver mode, notifications, language).
- **Communication & Chat:** Live room chat messages sent between room members during active sync sessions.

> [!NOTE]
> VibeSync uses **predefined account avatars** and does **not** collect or store personal photo uploads from your device.

---

## Data Usage

All collected information is strictly utilized to provide and enhance core VibeSync features:

- Facilitating synchronized media playback between room members
- Managing live watch rooms and participant lists
- Preserving personalized favorites, watch history, and account settings
- Maintaining room chat communication and active user state

We do **not** sell, rent, or monetize your personal data for advertising or marketing purposes.

---

## Data Security & Storage

- **Data Encryption:** All data transmitted between VibeSync and cloud services uses industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption protocols.
- **Persistence & Offline Security:** Account credentials, saved favorites, and user preferences are securely stored on your device with local encryption.
- **No Video Storage:** VibeSync streams media via official public interfaces. VibeSync does not download, host, or store third-party video content on its servers.

---

## No Data Selling

VibeSync **does not sell, rent, or share** personal user data with advertisers, third-party data brokers, or commercial entities.

---

## User Control & Account Deletion

You retain full control over your data within VibeSync:

- **Profile Modification:** Update your display name, handle (`@username`), and avatar at any time from the Profile tab.
- **Favorites & History Management:** Clear your watch history or remove items from your favorites directly within the application.
- **Account Deletion:** You can request account deletion or log out at any time. Logging out removes active session tokens from your device.

---

## Children's Privacy

VibeSync does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

---

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app enhancements or legal requirements. Updates will be reflected in this document with a revised "Last Updated" date.
